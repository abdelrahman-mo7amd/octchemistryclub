About OctChemClub

What is
October Chemistry Club?

OctChemistryClub exists to radically transform how young scientists engage with chemistry. We treat the subject not as memorizable facts, but as a living field of fascinating controversy, groundbreaking debate, and revolutionary discovery.

Through computational modeling, physical experimentation, and academic publishing, our members move beyond textbook contrainsts into real laboratories of scientific thought.

Critical Thinking Lab Experiments Research Publishing Competitions Peer Review Academic Writing

Research-First Culture

Every member is expectted to contribute original thought, whether through lab work, simulation, or written analysis.

Academic Publishing

Our magazine track publishes peer-reviewed articles written entirely by students, for students.

Competition Pipeline

CST and external competitons give members real pressure environments to sharpen their skills and earn recognition.
